AI & roboticsPrototype

MedShield

A sensor-monitoring prototype with a Flask API, SQLite storage, a hardware example, and an autoencoder that flags anomalous eight-value sensor readings trained on synthetic data.

  • Python
  • TensorFlow
  • Flask
  • SQLite
Important boundary +

This is an educational prototype, not a clinical device. Its simulator models normal and anomalous readings such as fever, falls, seizures, and humidity extremes.

Food Delivery Analysis

A two-part Python analysis of 2,050 synthetic food-delivery orders. It cleans the dataset, explores customer and payment behavior, evaluates restaurant and delivery patterns, and frames recommendations.

  • Python
  • Pandas
  • NumPy
  • Matplotlib
  • Seaborn
Observed dataset signals +

The locally stored dataset has 2,050 rows, a 33.4-minute average delivery time, Cairo as the most frequent city (984 orders), and Wallet as the most frequent payment method (699 orders).
